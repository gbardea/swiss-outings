In short
  • We only collect what the app needs to work for your family. No ads, no selling of data, no third-party tracking or analytics cookies.

1. What we store and why

DataWhy
Account: email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), first nameTo sign you in, keep your account safe and send account emails (confirmation, password reset).
Household: household name, members, invite codes, connected friendsSo you can plan together with your partner and see what friends are keen on.
Your picks: events you mark as Keen, Going or “Not for us”To show your plans, share them with your household and improve which events the nightly search looks for.
Activity in the app: e.g. which events you open, listen to, share or click through to, searches you type or speak, and plans you buildTo understand what's useful, fix problems and improve the app. Only the Swiss Outings team can see it.
Day planner (optional): your home address and its coordinates, number of adults, kids' ages, travel cards, saved day plansTo time trains, costs and stops for your family. Private to your household. A shared plan link shows the plan but never your address or family details.
Calendar (optional): a Google Calendar connection (free/busy only) or a private calendar link you pasteTo suggest days when you're free (“When can we go?”). Stored encrypted. We only read busy/free times, never event titles or details, and don't keep them. You can disconnect at any time in Settings.
Voice search (optional): a short recording when you tap the microphoneTurned into text so we can search for you. The recording isn't stored.
Messages: name, email and text you send via “Contact us” or “Suggest a place”To answer you and add the places you suggest.
Email preferences: whether you want the weekly “Swiss Outings Sunday” emailTo send it only if you want it. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.
Technical data: IP address, browser type, error logsProcessed briefly by our hosting provider to deliver the site and keep it secure.

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your login, email, picks, activity and calendar connection are removed. Shared household data (household settings and saved day plans) is removed when the last member of the household deletes their account. Messages you sent us may be kept as long as needed to answer them.

On your device, we store your sign-in session and a few settings (like your last view) in the browser's local storage. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

3. Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP/nDSG) and, where it applies, the EU GDPR, you can ask what we store about you, have it corrected or deleted, receive a copy, and object to its use. Most of this you can do yourself in the app. For anything else, write to us via Menu → Contact us or the email in the legal notice below. If you think we're getting it wrong, you can also contact the FDPIC (edoeb.admin.ch).

Swiss Outings is meant for adults planning outings for themselves and their families. We only store kids' ages as numbers, never their names.

4. About the events

Event details come from the organisers' public websites and are checked automatically every night. Times, prices and availability can change, so always confirm on the official page (linked on every event) before you travel or buy tickets. We're not responsible for third-party websites or events.

5. Changes

If we change how we handle data in an important way, we'll tell you in the app or by email. The date at the top shows the latest version.
