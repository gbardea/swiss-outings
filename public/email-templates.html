Supabase email templates

  1. In Supabase open Authentication → Emails → Templates and pick the template.
  2. Click Copy subject, paste into Subject.
  3. Click Copy email code, click into the Message body, press ⌘A then ⌘V. It must start with <!doctype html>.
  4. Click Save. The preview in Supabase should look like the one below.
